Star schema

Three facts share the conformed dimensions DimDate, DimCustomer and DimState: an item-grain transaction fact for revenue, an order-grain fact for delivery and review measures, and a payment-grain fact for the money mix. FactPayments also joins the other facts on OrderID.

Structured & unstructured data

Structured data

Nine relational CSV tables from Kaggle (orders, order items, payments, reviews, customers, sellers, products, category translation, geolocation). Every table was verified row-by-row against the canonical published sizes before use: orders 99,441 · order items 112,650 · payments 103,886 · reviews 99,224 · customers 99,441 · sellers 3,095 · products 32,951 · category translation 71 · geolocation 1,000,163.

Unstructured data

Free-text review comments in Portuguese, inside the reviews table: 39,073 of the delivered orders carry a written comment, with 14,162 distinct words after cleaning. For the word cloud, comments were lowercased, tokenised (words of 3+ letters) and stripped of Portuguese stopwords. The vocabulary is logistics, not products — produto (product) 17,237 mentions, prazo (deadline) 8,262, entrega (delivery) 6,206 — and comments mentioning antes (early) average 4.69 stars, against 3.60 for produto.

How the data was cleaned

  • Category names were translated from Portuguese to English using the source’s translation table; 610 products have no category in the source and are labelled unknown — kept visible, never dropped.
  • Review scores are averaged per order: the source holds 99,224 review rows for 98,673 orders — 551 orders carry two rows, and the prep averages them.
  • Derived in prep: DeliveryDays (delivered date − purchase timestamp), EstimatedDays, the IsLate flag, and per-order ItemCount, ItemRevenue, FreightTotal and PaymentTotal; surrogate keys were assigned to customers, sellers and products.
  • The facts keep all orders with an OrderStatus column — the delivered-only sales scope (96,478 of 99,441 orders) is applied in the DAX measures, not by deleting rows, so canceled and in-flight analysis stays possible.
  • Money reconciles: payments across delivered orders total R$15,422,462 ≈ items R$13,221,498 + freight R$2,198,276 (within 0.02%); the small residual is source rounding and voucher bookkeeping, kept as-is.
  • The geolocation table (1,000,163 rows) is deliberately not in the model: it is ZIP-prefix aggregates with many lat/lng samples per prefix — useful for mapping enrichment, not for sales facts. It ships in the project’s data/ folder for completeness.
  • A known source gap is kept as-is: November 2016 is near-empty and 2016 is a stub year (Sep–Dec, 267 delivered orders).
