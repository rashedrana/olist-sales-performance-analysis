Trend chart shows

Item revenue per month

The marketplace ramps through 2017 and spikes in November 2017 (Black Friday) — R$987,765 of revenue and 7,289 orders, +63% more orders than October. 2016 covers Sep–Dec only (with a near-empty November, a known gap in the source), and the delivered-order view ends in Aug 2018.

Revenue by customer state — top 12

Click a bar to make that state the main view. São Paulo alone is 38.3% of all revenue; the top 3 states hold 63.4%.

Revenue by product category

Click a bar to slice the whole dashboard to that category. Health & beauty leads on revenue (R$1.23M, 9.3%); bed, bath & table leads on orders (9,272). The top 10 of 74 categories take 62.4% of revenue.

Seasonality — revenue by calendar month

All months in the selected period combined. November towers over the year (Black Friday); December stays strong, January is the hangover.

Delivery speed vs review score

Bubble size = orders in the delivery-time band. Reviews fall steadily as delivery slows — and orders delivered late (after the promised date) average 2.57 stars vs 4.29 for on-time orders across the full dataset.

Payment mix — share of amount paid

Follows the period and state filters. Credit cards carry 78.5% of all money paid; the average credit-card order is split into 3.5 installments, and 67% of card orders use more than one.

When do people order? — by weekday

Monday is the busiest day (15,701 orders), Saturday the quietest (10,555); ordering peaks at 16:00 and nearly stops between 03:00 and 05:00.

Cumulative item revenue

In the full view, cumulative revenue crosses R$10M in May 2018 — 20 months after the first orders — and ends at R$13.2M.

Funnel — the order pipeline, all orders

Every order ever placed — this panel ignores the slicers by design, because the delivered-order scope would hide the leaks. 97.0% of orders end up delivered; the shipped → delivered gap is mostly orders still in transit at the October 2018 snapshot (1,107 with status “shipped”). Another 2,338 orders (2.4%) were neither delivered nor canceled at the snapshot — processing, invoiced, in transit or unavailable.

Waterfall — what moved revenue, 2017 → 2018

Delivered orders in the selected state (the period and category slicers do not apply — this panel is the 2017 → 2018 category bridge, and 2018 covers Jan–Aug purchases against the full 2017). The top 5 gaining and top 3 shrinking categories are shown individually; the other 66 categories are netted into one bar.

Word cloud — what customers write in reviews

Word size = frequency across the 39,073 written comments left on delivered orders (Portuguese stopwords removed; 14,162 distinct words). The vocabulary is delivery-obsessed — prazo (deadline) 8,262 mentions, antes (early) 5,592, chegou (arrived) 5,396 — and comments mentioning antes average 4.69 stars, vs 3.60 for comments about the produto itself.

Data source

Dataset: Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist (Kaggle), licence CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — downloaded from a public GitHub mirror and verified table by table against the canonical Kaggle release.
Coverage: 99,441 orders · 112,650 order items · 103,886 payment rows · 99,224 reviews · 32,951 products · 3,095 sellers. Orders placed 4 Sep 2016 – 17 Oct 2018; customers in all 27 Brazilian states.
Scope of the figures: delivered orders only — 96,478 of 99,441 (97.0%). The 625 canceled orders and the orders still in transit at the October 2018 snapshot are excluded, so purchase months run Sep 2016 – Aug 2018. Revenue is item price only; freight is reported separately (16.6% on top of item revenue).
Boundaries & model: state boundaries © IBGE (territorial meshes, simplified). The matching Power BI package — star schema (315,977 fact rows), DAX measures, page layout spec and 20 business questions, each answered with a different visual type — lives in this project under dashboard/powerbi/.
